BEFORE YOU BOOK

Booking & Court Rules

Please take a moment to read these rules. They help every booking start safely and on time.

1
⏰ Arrive on time
Please arrive 10 minutes early to warm up. Your slot begins and ends at the exact times you booked.
2
🚫 No cancellations after booking
Cancellations must be made at least 6 hours before your scheduled time. No-shows and late cancellations are non-refundable.
3
🔄 Rebooking policy
Rebooking requests must be made at least 6 hours in advance and are subject to court availability. Contact us at 0976-040-2293 to rebook.
4
🌧️ Weather cancellations
This is an outdoor court. In case of rain or a natural calamity, we will contact you to reschedule at no extra charge.
5
💰 Payment required to confirm
Upload your GCash or cash payment screenshot to secure your slot. Your booking is only confirmed upon receipt of payment.
6
🤝 Respect the court & players
Be kind to all players. Vacate the court promptly when your time is up so the next booking can start on time.
